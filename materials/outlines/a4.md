Part 1 covers the meter each engine runs on: Athena bytes ($5.00/TB, 10 MB min), Lambda time (GB-second $0.0000166667 + $0.20 per 1M requests, billed per 1 ms), Fargate always-on (vCPU-hour $0.04048, GB-hour $0.004445), Redshift Serverless RPU-hour $0.375, per second with a 60 s minimum, nothing when idle. Rule of thumb: spiky and short -> functions; steady and long -> containers; queries -> per-byte or warehouse (session 6 analyst). Figure: cost lines vs invocations per day with the crossover.
